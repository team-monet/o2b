# Guidance

Governing parent: [BRAIN.md](../BRAIN.md)

Read this guide at session start. Behavioural guidance has one central home under `guidance/`, including guidance scoped to particular projects.

The personal brain may contain:

- `PRINCIPLES.md`: shared and explicitly scoped principles and preferences.
- `STAGES.md`: action moments, applicability hints, and links to rule files.
- `stages/<stage>.md`: the rules for one action moment, grouped by applicability.

The framework supplies conventions, not populated personal guidance. If an optional file is absent, do not infer its contents.

## Apply guidance

Apply only guidance whose project, actor, domain, and action scope matches the task. A scope heading governs the entries beneath it until the next scope heading; entry qualifiers may narrow it. Match stages by the meaning of the moment, not exact wording, and follow BRAIN's read-before-action instruction.

A **Blocking** rule is a boundary; do not cross it outside its authorization conditions. Follow **Advisory** guidance unless you state the departure and why. A missing rationale does not retire admitted guidance or change its authority.

## Maintain guidance

Before creating or changing guidance, read [Maintain guidance](authoring.md). Its authoring, admission, and migration instructions are not part of routine startup reading.
